About 404 Cinema

404 Cinema opened its doors as a small university project turned into an idea we actually wanted to build for real: a cinema that doesn't make you choose between a good screen, good sound and a seat that doesn't hurt your back after two hours.

We started with one auditorium and a single 4K projector. Today we run three very different halls — IMAX Laser, a VIP Lounge and a dedicated Dolby Atmos room — so there's a format for whatever mood you're in.

Why People Keep Coming Back

  • Real IMAX laser projection, not just a marketing label
  • Student discounts every day of the week
  • Online booking so you skip the ticket line
  • Staff who actually answer the phone
