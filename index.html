Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers to common questions about ticket bookings, discounts, and theater policies.

Online ticket reservations can be refunded or exchanged up to 2 hours before the movie screening begins. Simply enter your booking reference code in your profile or show your digital voucher at the box office desk.
The student discount applies Monday through Friday to all Standard 2D, 3D, and IMAX sessions. Simply select the student checkbox during booking and present your valid university student ID card at the auditorium entrance.
Club members get 10% bonus cashback on every ticket, free popcorn refills on Thursdays, and exclusive access to premiere pre-screenings with VIP recliner seating.
Guests may bring standard personal drinks and light snacks. Hot prepared meals, fast food, and glass containers are strictly prohibited inside our laser auditoriums to preserve guest comfort and seat upholstery.
