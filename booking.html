Book Your Seat Online

No more waiting in line at the box office. Pick a movie, choose where you want to sit and we'll have your ticket ready for you at the counter.

How Booking Works

  1. Pick the movie you want to watch from the list below.
  2. Choose your row — Front, Middle or our VIP row.
  3. Fill in your name, email and any extra comment for us.
  4. Press "Book Now" and we'll send a confirmation to your email.

Tip: seats in the VIP row sell out fastest on weekends, so book a few days ahead if you can.
